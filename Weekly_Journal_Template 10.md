# Week 10: Understanding the Importance of Codebooks

**Date:** March 25, 2026  
**Phase:** Architect  
**Approach Used:** Connector

## Your Reflection

This week’s work on the Codebook and Qualitative Memo helped me better understand how important structure and clarity are when creating a research portfolio. Before this, I didn’t really think about how much planning goes into research behind the scenes. I used to assume that researchers mostly just collected data and explained it, but now I see that a lot of the work happens before analysis even begins.

Creating the codebook showed me how necessary it is to define variables clearly and consistently. If the definitions are vague, then the results can become unreliable because different people might interpret the same data in different ways. This connects directly to the idea of producing strong, credible research. It also made me realize that a codebook is not just a requirement for an assignment—it’s like a rulebook that ensures accuracy and consistency throughout the entire project.

The qualitative memo also helped me reflect on my own thinking process. Writing it forced me to slow down and explain why I made certain coding decisions instead of just doing them. This connects to real-world research because professionals need to justify their methods and be transparent about their reasoning.

Overall, this week made me see how these skills apply beyond just this class. Whether I’m working on future academic research or even professional projects, being organized and intentional with my approach will make my work stronger and more trustworthy.
